Rivia Privacy Policy

Rivia is a personal assistant app used by its owner only. When you connect Google Calendar, Rivia requests read-only access (calendar.readonly) to read your calendar events so it can plan your schedule.

Calendar data is processed on the owner's own device and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Rivia's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
